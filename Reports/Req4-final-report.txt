Findings
- None of the named policy capabilities is exposed at the probed routes in the current mock. This is a Req4 coverage gap; detailed business or validation defects cannot be established from this requirement.
- Empty-body, missing-body, unknown-ID, and nominal behavior remain unassessed because route handling did not reach a policy implementation.
- No fraud-rule violation can be identified because Req4 provides no fraud rules.

Recommendations
1. Confirm the intended API methods and paths, then implement and mount Create, Update, Cancel, and Fetch Policy operations.
2. Define policy request/response fields, required-field and format validation, authentication/authorization, state transitions, idempotency, and HTTP/error behavior.
3. Rerun these 12 probes after the endpoints are available and replace route-characterization assertions with contract-based functional assertions.
4. Add fraud scenarios only if policy-related fraud controls are explicitly specified.
