Requirement
Reject a claim when both strict conditions hold:
- Claim Amount > INR 100,000
- Policy Age < 30 days

Findings
- The tested legacy POST /claims endpoint accepts the policyAgeDays field but does not evaluate it. Its implemented rules compare claim amount to policyLimit, check policyStatus, and apply claim-type thresholds; it does not enforce the Req1 amount-and-age combination.
- All three cases meeting both INS-1456 conditions were returned as ACCEPTED instead of REJECTED. The seven cases not meeting both conditions were not rejected, consistent with this rule's expected result only.
- The response contract for rejection (HTTP status, body status, and reason field) is not defined by the requirement. This test asserts body status REJECTED as the business decision; HTTP 201 is the observed creation response.
- Policy-age calculation reference date and calendar-day versus elapsed-day semantics are unspecified.
- This run used synthetic data and does not confirm real-world fraud.

Recommendations
1. Implement the combined rule on the claim-processing endpoint: claimAmount > 100000 AND policyAgeDays < 30 must result in rejection.
2. Define how policy age is computed and document the rejection response contract.
3. Rerun all 10 cases after implementation, retaining exact-threshold and single-condition-false tests as regression coverage.
